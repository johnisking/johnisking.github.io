Privacy Policy

Sperm Race — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · App ID: com.spermrace.game

Who we are

Sperm Race is made by JOHNISKING (존이스킹), a sole proprietorship run by Jaehyun Cho in Seosan, Chungnam, South Korea. This policy covers the Android app on Google Play, the browser version, and the Facebook & Messenger Instant Games version.

Contact for any privacy question: yunany90@gmail.com

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your email, phone number or address.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your own device.
  • Multiplayer sends only the nickname you type and your position in the current race, and the room is deleted automatically.
  • We send ourselves anonymous gameplay statistics so we can improve the game. They cannot identify you.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally (browser localStorage, or app storage on Android). It never leaves your device unless stated otherwise below:

  • Level progress and how many rounds you have played
  • Your chosen language, sound setting, control scheme and screen orientation
  • Your sperm's appearance (colour and shape)
  • The nickname you type for multiplayer, and a random room-join identifier that is not linked to any real identity
  • Whether you have purchased the ad-free unlock

Anonymous gameplay statistics

While you are online, the game sends a small amount of anonymous usage data to our game server (a Cloudflare Worker) so we can see which levels are too hard and whether the game is working. Each record contains:

  • A random anonymous ID created on your device, plus a session ID. Neither is tied to your name, your Facebook account, your phone number or your advertising ID.
  • The platform (Android app, Facebook, or browser), the game version and your language setting
  • Events such as starting a level, finishing a level, the result and how long it took
  • Error reports: if the game hits a bug, the error message and where in the code it came from

We use this only in aggregate, to improve the game. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it cannot be used to identify you. Clearing the app or browser data removes the anonymous ID, and a new unrelated one is created next time.

Bug reports

The game has a "Report a bug" screen you can use if you want to. It is never sent automatically. When you send one, it includes the text you wrote, the contact detail you chose to type (optional), and basic technical information — your language, browser information and screen size — so we can reproduce the problem. We use it only to fix the bug.

Multiplayer

When you create or join a room, the game connects to our game server (a Cloudflare Worker). While the room is open, the server holds:

  • The nickname you entered (maximum 16 characters)
  • Your position, direction and speed in the current race, so other players can see you move
  • Round results — finishing times and placements

This exists only to run the match. Rooms are deleted automatically two hours after the last activity, and nothing is kept afterwards. We do not log IP addresses for analytics, and multiplayer data is not linked to any advertising identifier.

Facebook & Messenger Instant Games

When you play inside Facebook or Messenger, the game uses the Facebook Instant Games SDK version 8 ("Zero Permissions"). In this version the game does not receive your name or your profile picture, and it does not request access to your Facebook profile, your friends or your posts. Nothing is ever posted to your account.

The multiplayer nickname is simply the name you type in yourself, handled exactly as described in the Multiplayer section above.

Advertising and purchases

Android (Google Play)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and device information to serve and measure ads. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Purchases of the ad-free unlock are handled by Google Play Billing — we never see your card details.

Facebook & Messenger

Ads are served through Meta Audience Network on the Instant Games platform, under Meta's Privacy Policy, and those ads may use device or session identifiers held by Meta. The Facebook version does not sell anything — there is no in-game purchase and no payment step.

Browser version

The itch.io web version has no ads and no purchases.

Children

Sperm Race is a comedy racing game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because there is no account, you control everything from your device. Full instructions are on the data deletion page. In short: clearing the app or browser data removes everything stored locally along with the anonymous statistics ID, and multiplayer rooms expire on their own. You may also email us and we will confirm what, if anything, remains.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.

한국어 요약

스펌레이스(Sperm Race)는 계정이 없습니다. 이메일·전화번호·주소를 묻지 않으며, 진행 상황과 설정은 모두 이용자 기기 안에만 저장됩니다.

기기에 저장되는 것

레벨 진행도, 플레이 횟수, 언어·소리·조작 방식·화면 방향 설정, 정자 꾸미기, 멀티플레이용 닉네임과 임의의 방 참가 식별자, 광고 제거 구매 여부.

멀티플레이

방에 들어가 있는 동안에만 닉네임(최대 16자), 현재 경기의 위치·방향·속도, 순위 기록이 게임 서버(Cloudflare Worker)에 보관됩니다. 마지막 활동으로부터 2시간이 지나면 방은 자동으로 삭제되며 이후 아무것도 남지 않습니다.

익명 플레이 통계

게임을 개선하려고, 인터넷에 연결되어 있을 때 익명 통계를 게임 서버로 보냅니다. 기기에서 만든 무작위 익명 ID와 세션 ID, 플랫폼·버전·언어, 스테이지 시작·클리어 같은 이벤트, 그리고 오류가 났을 때의 오류 메시지가 들어갑니다. 이름·페이스북 계정·전화번호·광고 식별자와 연결되지 않으며, 광고에 쓰거나 판매하지 않습니다. 앱·브라우저 데이터를 지우면 익명 ID도 함께 지워집니다.

버그 신고

게임 안의 「버그 신고」로 직접 보낼 때만 전송됩니다. 작성한 내용, 선택 입력한 연락처, 언어·브라우저 정보·화면 크기가 담기며 문제를 재현하는 데만 씁니다.

페이스북 인스턴트 게임

페이스북·메신저 버전은 인스턴트 게임 SDK 8.0(제로 권한)을 사용합니다. 게임이 이름이나 프로필 사진을 받지 않으며, 프로필·친구·게시물 접근을 요청하지 않습니다. 페이스북에 글을 올리는 일도 없습니다. 멀티플레이 닉네임은 이용자가 직접 입력한 이름입니다.

광고·결제

안드로이드 버전은 Google AdMob 광고를 사용하고, 결제는 Google Play 결제로 처리되어 카드 정보를 저희가 보지 않습니다. 페이스북 버전의 광고는 Meta Audience Network를 통해 제공되며, 페이스북 버전에는 판매하는 상품이 없어 결제 단계가 없습니다. itch.io 웹 버전은 광고와 결제가 없습니다.
